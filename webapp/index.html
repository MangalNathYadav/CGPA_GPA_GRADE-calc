📈 How Relative Grading Works

In relative grading, your grade depends on how you perform relative to your classmates, rather than against fixed percentage cutoffs. The system uses the class mean (average) and standard deviation (σ) to set dynamic grade boundaries.

Grade Boundary Meaning
S ≥ Mean + 1.5σ Top performers, well above average
A ≥ Mean + 1.0σ Significantly above average
B+ ≥ Mean + 0.5σ Above average
B ≥ Mean At the class average
C+ ≥ Mean − 0.5σ Slightly below average
C ≥ Mean − 1.0σ Below average
D ≥ Mean − 1.5σ Significantly below average
E ≥ Mean − 2.0σ Far below average
F Below Fail

💡 Tip: If you don't have the standard deviation, provide the highest and lowest marks in the class. We'll estimate σ ≈ (highest − lowest) / 4 for you.
